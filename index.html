The Problem

Employer Costs Are
Rising Fast

Hypothesis 1
🌇 The 8:30 AM Plug-In Problem
Employees arrive at work, plug in, and charge for the full workday. Simultaneous morning plug-ins spike the peak 15-minute kW demand — raising monthly bills dramatically. Sessions after 2 PM running 6+ hours extend into the costly 4–9 PM peak rate window.
Hypothesis 2
💷 Free Charging Amplifies the Problem
When employers absorb 100% of electricity costs, employees have no price signal to moderate behavior — leading to disproportionately long, high-energy sessions that spike demand charges.

Feature Importance (Model Contribution)
Why These 7?
Station IDs and driver IDs were excluded — we wanted behavioral signals available before a session starts, not identifiers.

The target threshold (≥14.31 kWh) represents the top 25th percentile of session energy — the sessions that actually drive employer costs.

All 7 features are knowable or estimable at plug-in time, enabling real-time scoring.

Why AUC over Accuracy?
Our target is imbalanced (25% positive). A naïve model predicting "never high-demand" is 75% accurate but useless. AUC penalizes this and rewards true discrimination.
